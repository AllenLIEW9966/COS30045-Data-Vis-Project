NATIONAL & STATE TRENDS

Hospitalisation trends by jurisdiction

COMBINED LINE CHART ↗

i The Australia line is the published national total. State and territory counts do not necessarily sum to it.

Key finding. National hospitalisations rose from 34,024 in 2012 to 39,505 in 2021, an increase of 16.1%. Jurisdictions followed different paths; Victoria recorded more hospitalisations than New South Wales in 2021.

This chart starts in 2012 because the prepared Question 1 workbook contains 2012–2021. The BITRE source publication also covers 2011; that year is outside this chart’s prepared series.

GEOGRAPHIC REMOTENESS

Hospitalisations by residential remoteness

Most hospitalisations were among residents of Major Cities. The published “Other or unknown” category stays visible in the distribution.

DONUT DISTRIBUTION ↗

i Counts are based on the recorded remoteness area. The table reports “Other or unknown” separately from Major Cities, Regional and Remote.

Key finding. Major Cities accounted for 26,355 of the 39,505 hospitalisations in 2021, about two-thirds of the national total. This is a count of admissions, not a measure of individual risk.
