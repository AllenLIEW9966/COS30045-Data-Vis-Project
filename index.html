ABOUT THE DATA

Read the counts in context.

These charts describe hospitalised road crash injury counts, not all crashes or population-adjusted risk. The national, state and territory, and First Nations workbooks report data from the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare National Hospital Morbidity Database.

Source workbooks: hospitalisation_injury_publication_sep2023.xlsx; state-and-territory-hospitalisation-injury-publication-2023.xlsx; First-Nations-hospitalised-injuries-2023.xlsx.
