Method, data & design decisions

Where every number comes from, how it was reshaped, and why each chart was chosen. All data is real — nothing was invented, estimated or filled in.

Data sources

  • A CORGIS Video Games dataset — 1,212 titles released 2004–2008 across five platforms, with units sold, review score, genre, publisher, ESRB rating and HowLongToBeat playtimes. Fetched from corgis-edu.github.io.
  • B SteamSpy API — live snapshot of 2,953 Steam titles with concurrent players, owner estimates, review counts and price. Pulled from steamspy.com/api.php.
The Steam figures are a point-in-time snapshot, not a live feed. Re-run the fetch script to refresh them.

Derived files

  • 1platform_year.csv — sales, titles, mean score and mean length per platform per year.
  • 2genre_year_platform.csv — the three-way rollup driving the heatmap.
  • 3games.csv — one row per title for the scatter plot.
  • 4publisher_year.csv — top-10 publishers by total sales.
  • 5steam_games.csv, steam_publishers.csv, steam_price_buckets.csv — Steam rollups.

Multi-genre strings like Action,Role-Playing (RPG) are reduced to their primary (first) genre. Owner ranges such as 100,000,000 .. 200,000,000 are converted to their midpoint.

Why the race animates properly

A bar race is only interesting if the bars actually move and change order. This one plots cumulative sales, which always grows, and interpolates between years on a requestAnimationFrame loop rather than snapping from one year to the next. The result is continuous motion at any speed.

A crown marks the current leader, and when the lead changes hands a banner fires. Era captions are keyed to real hardware launches so the animation tells a story rather than just moving rectangles.

Chart rationale

  • 1Bar race — rank change over time is the whole story of a console war.
  • 2Stacked area / lines — stacked shows total market size, lines allow direct comparison. Toggle rather than pick one.
  • 3Heatmap — the natural encoding for a genre × platform matrix.
  • 4Scatter — the only honest way to show a weak correlation; a bar chart would hide the spread.
  • 5Ranked bars with colour-encoded rating — two variables (popularity and reception) in one readable chart.

Running it

No build step. Because charts use d3.csv(), the folder must be served over HTTP — opening the file from disk shows a warning screen instead of empty charts. 

cd "Allen GameDV"
python -m http.server 8000
# open http://localhost:8000

Or just double-click START HERE.bat.

Limitations

The CORGIS dataset covers 2004–2008 only, so this is a study of the seventh console generation rather than the whole industry. It is also skewed toward titles that received Metacritic coverage, meaning very small releases are under-represented. Steam owner counts are SteamSpy estimates derived from review ratios, not figures published by Valve, and should be read as order-of-magnitude rather than exact.
