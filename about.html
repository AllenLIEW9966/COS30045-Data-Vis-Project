SOURCES & INTERPRETATION

Read the counts
in context.

The charts describe hospitalised road crash injuries in Australia. Annual jurisdiction counts cover 2012–2021; the other breakdowns show 2021.

WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

Hospitalisations are counts.

They do not represent every crash, every injury, or population-adjusted risk. Comparisons between groups and places need population denominators before they can be interpreted as rates.

The published Australia total is retained separately from state and territory figures; the reported jurisdictions do not necessarily add to the national figure.

COVERAGE NOTES

Keep the source limits visible.

  • The age-by-sex rows sum to 39,504, one below the published 2021 Australia total of 39,505.
  • The remoteness distribution retains the published “Other or unknown” category.
  • The First Nations chart compares counts. Its unknown-area values are residuals derived from group totals less the three reported areas. The two group totals sum to 38,975; 530 cases fall outside them.
